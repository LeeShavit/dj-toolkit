SETLIST ID – find every track in a DJ set
==========================================

START IT
  Mac:     double-click "Setlist ID.command"
  Windows: double-click "Setlist ID.bat"

  A small window opens, and then a page in your web browser.
  The very first time, it spends a minute or two setting itself up.

USE IT
  1. Copy the link to a DJ set on SoundCloud or YouTube.
  2. Paste it into the box and press Go.
  3. Wait. A one-hour set takes about 15–20 minutes. Leave the page open.
  4. The tracklist opens on its own, with links to Spotify, Apple Music,
     Deezer, YouTube, SoundCloud, Beatport and more for every track.

  Your old tracklists stay listed on the start page.
  To stop the app, press "Quit Setlist ID" at the bottom of the page,
  or close the small window.

FIRST TIME ONLY – if your computer blocks it
  Mac: you may see "Apple could not verify Setlist ID.command".
    Click Done, then open System Settings → Privacy & Security,
    scroll down, and click "Open Anyway" next to Setlist ID.command.
  Windows: you may see "Windows protected your PC".
    Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

GOOD TO KNOW
  - Tracks marked "possible" were only heard once – they might be wrong.
  - "ID – unidentified" means the track isn't in Shazam's database
    (often unreleased music).
  - It needs an internet connection while it works.
  - Free to use: no accounts or payments are involved.
  - To remove it, delete this folder.
